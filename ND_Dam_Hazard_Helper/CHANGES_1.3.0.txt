ND Dam Hazard Classification Helper - 1.3.0 (hazard classification)
===================================================================

NEW: HAZARD CLASSIFICATION TAB
- Habitable structures
  * Sources: OSM building outlines (OSM_Outlines.gpkg), NSI / FEMA /
    ND GIS Hub point layers in the project, and an optional USACE National
    Structure Inventory (NSI) download for the flooded extent.
  * Points inside an outline describe that building; points from different
    sources within 30 ft are merged; duplicates are removed.
  * Habitability per ND REG_05.2026 5.2.1.1; outbuildings excluded;
    uncertain buildings reported as "Unverified" for review.
  * Mobile home vs house on foundation from NSI RES2 / building type H,
    OSM static_caravan, or manufactured/mobile/trailer attributes.
  * Zone per structure from depth and velocity in the SAME cell, over the
    outline plus the 5-ft buffer, using that structure's curve; the worst
    cell governs. (The 1.2 edge-buffer fields combined the maximum depth
    and maximum velocity, which can come from different cells.)
- Roads: overtopped stretches (10-ft sampling), road class, paved status,
  bridge flag, most recent AADT (road layer incl. AADT_yyyy columns, an
  optional AADT layer, FRA crossing AADT), ND class, TM 11 motorists.
- Rail: national rail network lines and FRA grade crossings downloaded for
  the area (or your own layers); trains/day from same-railroad crossings
  within 3 miles; passenger lines flagged.
- Classification: ND Dam Safety Standards Policy REG_05.2026 (effective
  04/01/2026) Section 5.2, and ACER TM 11 Table 1 by lives in jeopardy.
  The summary lists the reasons and the items needing engineering judgment.
- Outputs: Habitable_Structures, Roads_Inundated, Rail_Inundated
  (GPKG/CSV/XLSX, styled), Hazard_Classification_Summary (TXT/XLSX/CSV),
  dated download cache in outputs/_downloads.
- Self-test extended (11 new checks).

PENDING
- ACER TM 11 Figure 4 (passenger vehicles): the curve coefficients are not
  yet included, so road vehicle zones and TM 11 motorist counts are
  skipped (the summary says so). ND road criteria do not need this curve.

LIMITS TO KEEP IN MIND
- AADT comes from the layers you provide plus FRA crossings; local roads
  without counts are listed for review. The plugin does not query NDDOT's
  servers directly - add their count layer (or HPMS) to the project.
- A 2D depth grid shows water under bridges; bridge stretches are flagged
  and need confirmation.
- Sole-access roads, lifeline facilities, imminent development and dams
  in series (ND 5.2.1.2, 5.2.2, 5.3) still need the engineer's review.
