    3.26.2 - With ND Dam Hazard 2.34.4 and ND Dam Breach Screening 0.32.4. LISFLOOD-FP virtual gauges: the gauge section is clipped to the model grid. It was as long as the domain's longest side and laid across the narrow side, so it ran off the grid; LISFLOOD-FP's own clip compares the wrong axis, so it read past its flux arrays and the gauge discharge could be garbage (1e297, or a negative value shown as 0) depending on the build. Gauge discharges of earlier runs may be wrong.
    3.26.1 - With ND Dam Hazard 2.34.3 and ND Dam Breach Screening 0.32.3. HEC-RAS import: with the model terrain given, depth is the HEC-RAS cell's maximum water surface minus the terrain averaged onto each output grid cell (was minus the cell's minimum elevation, which gave a ditch's depth to the whole cell), and the wet area and arrival time follow that depth (arrival per grid cell, interpolated, else the time of the maximum); without a terrain it is unchanged, with a note. The depth basis is recorded. New 'hecras-reexport' command re-makes the rasters of a finished plan HDF without re-running HEC-RAS. With a terrain, HEC-RAS depths and flooded areas go down on coarse cells.
    3.26.0 - With ND Dam Hazard 2.34.3 and ND Dam Breach Screening 0.32.3. Two changes from the Absaraka ND-vs-DamSafety accuracy check. (1) The model grid now stays in the DEM's horizontal CRS when the DEM is projected in metres (a 3DEP download is metres in EPSG:5070), so the result rasters are not reprojected afterwards: reprojecting a UTM model grid back to the DEM's CRS left no-data triangles on every raster edge, which made the hazard model-extent check report "the flood does not reach the edge" while the edges were wet. A named model CRS is still used only when the DEM has no projected metre CRS. (2) The run report and the run summary now give the share of the breach volume that left the model through its edges (LISFLOOD-FP boundary outflow), and warn when it is 5% or more: the domain grows only while an edge is deeper than 1 ft, so a wide, shallow sheet could carry a large share of the volume out with nothing to say so, understating the flood area and the downstream consequences. No breach parameter or flood-solver default changed.

  3.22.0 - With ND Dam Hazard 2.32.0, ND Dam Breach Screening 0.30.0 and ND Data Updater 1.6.0 (damsuite 1.18.0). A new tool and two fixes. (1) NEW TOOL 'Spillway adequacy': does the dam pass its inflow design flood with NO breach? It routes each inflow hydrograph in a folder (e.g. the PMF from the rainfall tool) through the reservoir, releasing through the spillway rating, any outlets or gates, and over the crest once the pool tops the dam, and reports whether the peak pool stays the required freeboard below the top of dam (REG_05.2026 Sec. 8 / 9: high and medium dams max(1 ft, wind set-up + run-up), low dams 3 ft), plus the largest multiple of the inflow the dam passes. This is the question a breach run cannot answer: a peak-triggered breach below the top of dam only models a failure there, it does not find one, and the breach report now says so and points here. (2) The breach inflow point is kept on the DOWNSTREAM side of the crest and out of the reservoir: on a dry flood-control dam the lowest ground near the dam point can be the empty reservoir floor, and the breach was then fed back into the reservoir instead of released downstream (found on Absaraka DWR-2801). The downstream side is taken from the pool behind the dam, the crest line's normal, or the downstream trace, in that order. (3) The shared suite-settings file is written safely (atomic replace with a .bak, a one-writer lock, and a merge that keeps another plugin's keys), so two plugins saving at once cannot corrupt it. Breach and flood numbers for a dam already modelled do not move unless its inflow point was on the wrong side of the crest.
  3.21.0 - With ND Dam Hazard 2.31.0, ND Dam Breach Screening 0.29.0 and ND Data Updater 1.5.0 (damsuite 1.17.0). Three fixes.   3.20.3 - With ND Dam Hazard 2.30.3, ND Dam Breach Screening 0.28.3 and ND Data Updater 1.4.3 (damsuite 1.16.3). Three fixes from the ND vs DamSafety comparison of Absaraka Dam. (1) A TOP OF DAM THIS PLUGIN ESTIMATED from the DEM is written to dam.json as an estimate (datum 'DEM estimate') and every later run estimates it again from its own DEM, instead of reading one run's guess back as a given crest - which also let the breach inflow point move to the crest and RAISE_CREST raise the dam line to it. A value you gave, or another tool measured, is read back as before. (2) The window around the dam point is read at the DEM's OWN pixel size when that is coarser than 3 m: resampling a 40 m DEM to 3 m invented a smooth slope over the embankment and read the crest about 10 ft low. A run that estimates the crest from a DEM coarser than 5 m now says the estimate is probably low and asks for the top of dam or the dam crest line. (3) A GeoJSON product keeps its CRS when the working CRS is WKT (a custom or ESRI CRS): the EPSG code is looked up, and a CRS with no code goes beside the file as a .prj. Such files used to go out unlabelled, which QGIS and ArcGIS read as longitude / latitude. The report and the tool help now say which statistic the crest estimate used (the 98th percentile within 30 m, or the median along the crest line) instead of 'the highest DEM point'. A run with a given top of dam, or a 1-10 m DEM, is unchanged.
